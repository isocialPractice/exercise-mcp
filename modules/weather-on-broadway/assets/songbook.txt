# Weather on Broadway - Original Songbook
#
# Original pastiche numbers written for this exercise, one per weather mood.
# Any resemblance to a real showtune is strictly rhythmic - no licensed
# lyrics appear in this file.
#
# Format, parsed by src/songbook.ts:
#   --SONG: <MOOD>--
#   Title: <show title>
#   Tempo: <how the pit plays it>
#   Chorus:
#   <chorus lines until the end marker>
#   --END--

--SONG: SUNNY--
Title: Marquee Morning
Tempo: up-tempo opener, 4/4 strut
Chorus:
Oh the sun is up on the marquee lights,
Every cloud has cleared the wings,
It's a gold-leaf, top-bill, opening-night
Kind of day where the whole town sings!
--END--

--SONG: CLOUDY--
Title: Gray Matinee
Tempo: wistful waltz, 3/4
Chorus:
It's a gray matinee, pull the houselights low,
Soft as velvet seats worn thin,
There's a quiet kind of show in the overcast row
When the clouds come shuffling in.
--END--

--SONG: STORMY--
Title: Thunder on Forty-Second
Tempo: dramatic act-one finale, 6/8
Chorus:
Thunder on Forty-Second, lightning off the fly rail,
Rain across the footlights - hold your hats and hold the show!
The percussion's in the heavens and the strings are in the gale,
It's the loudest number this old street has ever known!
--END--

--SONG: SNOWY--
Title: Powder and Footlights
Tempo: gentle ballad, slow 4/4
Chorus:
Powder on the footlights, hush across the square,
Every flake a spotlight drifting down,
The city wears an encore of white upon the air,
And the snow takes one more bow across the town.
--END--

--SONG: ALERT--
Title: Hold the Curtain!
Tempo: urgent ensemble number, cut time
Chorus:
Hold the curtain! Clear the stage!
The weather's gone and stolen every scene!
Read the playbill, heed the page -
This bulletin's the biggest cue you've seen!
--END--
